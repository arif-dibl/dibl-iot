Technical Specifications

Input Voltage 220V AC Mains (AC IN terminal, 50Hz)
Relay Outputs 4 pairs of 220V AC output terminals (1, 2, 3, 4)
Max Relay Load AC 220V, Max 1 HP per channel. (Use magnetic contactor for loads > 1 HP).
Sensor Ports 4x RJ45 Sockets (S1, S2, S3, S4). Supports standard CAT6 cables.
Sensor Cable Range Up to 20m for Soil Moisture & Temp-Humidity; up to 1,200m for 7-in-1 Nutrition Sensor.
Built-in Sensors Device Temperature (°C/°F), Device Humidity (%RH), and Light Level.
Display & Controls Onboard dashboard screen, 1x Scroll/OK knob, 1x Menu/Back button, 4x manual output buttons.
Indicators 3 status LEDs (Power/WiFi, Server, Timer) + 4 green output indicators.
Wireless Protocol Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz (AP Setup Mode: 192.168.4.1)
Timers 12 weekly schedule timers with weekday & output channel selection.
Operating Range -25°C to +70°C | Device: 45%–80% RH | External Sensors: 0%–100% RH
Data Logging 6 Months cloud history with CSV export via my.senspanel.com

Setup & Wiring

Note: Refer to the included User Manual for full safety guide, 10 AWG wire recommendations, and step-by-step diagrams.
1
Wiring (AC IN & Loads)

Connect 220V to AC IN. Wire loads (max 1 HP) to outputs 1–4. Plug CAT6 sensors into S1–S4.

2
Wi-Fi (192.168.4.1)

Connect phone to device Wi-Fi, open 192.168.4.1 in browser, and enter farm Wi-Fi info.

3
Link Account

Log in to my.senspanel.com and scan the device QR code to link.
